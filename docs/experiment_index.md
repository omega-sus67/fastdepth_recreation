Use this page to find a particular question and its evidence. The [four-experiment guide](learning/four_experiments.md) teaches the central ideas; the [progress report](progress_report.md) retains the complete dated history and failures. ## Pruning and recovery

**Scene-aware proposal and controls:** [learning guide](learning/scene_aware_pruning.md), [fixed protocol](../configs/experiment_2026-11-14_scene_aware.json), [32-named-scene channel scores](../results/training/2026-11-14-scene-aware-scores.json), [paired non-deterministic results](../results/evaluation/2026-11-14-scene-aware-paired-summary.json), and [desktop TensorRT output differences](../results/evaluation/2026-11-14-scene-tensorrt-outliers.json). The original 0.001448 mean-image tuning RMSE gap between scene-aware and average-ablation was smaller than a 0.002003 same-seed replay variation for the original model. In the [deterministic-mode repeat](../results/evaluation/2026-11-14-deterministic-scene-paired.json), that three-seed mean gap narrowed to 0.000141, and the trained original's mean was 0.000131 lower than scene-aware. See [current priorities](immediate_priority.md) and [progress history](progress_report.md) for limitations. No reliable improvement, independent generalization, novelty or Orin speedup is established.

**Newer multi-scene tuning comparison:** [protocol](../configs/experiment_2026-11-14_new_tuning_comparison.json), [ten-model summary with 26 named scenes](../results/evaluation/2026-11-14-new-tuning-comparison/summary.json), [per-image results](../results/evaluation/2026-11-14-new-tuning-comparison/per_image.csv), [aggregation verification](../results/evaluation/2026-11-14-new-tuning-aggregation-check.json), and [desktop timing](../results/benchmarks/2026-11-14-new-model-comparison.json). The original achieved mean per-image tuning RMSE 0.300091 versus 0.306396 for the closest existing candidate, weight-selected 192 before recovery. The larger tuning sample comes from NYU training shards and may overlap the community checkpoint's unknown pretraining data; it has now been inspected and is not a final test. The ten checkpoints were created earlier, not trained on the new 1,857-image training split. See the [walkthrough](learning/four_experiments.md#comparison-on-the-newer-445-image-tuning-set--14-november) for the complete comparison.

The earlier exploratory comparison below uses the [expanded 128-image training / 64-image tuning manifest](../data/nyu_depth_v2/multiscene_2026-11-14-expanded/manifest.json). Its tuning set has only two scenes and has been inspected repeatedly. The local 654-file evaluation collection was not used to choose these pruning candidates. Values below are **mean per-image tuning RMSE in native units**, lower is better.
